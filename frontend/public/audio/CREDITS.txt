CREATURE AUDIO — added 2026-09-24
These are real voice performances, dog recordings, insects and a gas-torch recording,
edited for fictional creatures. They are NOT recordings of real zombies, and not
audio extracted from Hunt: Showdown. The earlier oscillator-generated sounds were removed.

Normal, Armored, Hive and Immolator voice layers:
"Zombie/Monster roars, growls, grunts, devouring sounds (juicy)" by umnachtung.
https://freesound.org/people/umnachtung/sounds/533165/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: short segment extraction, mono conversion, normalization, fades,
moderate pitch changes and mixing with the CC0 recordings listed below.
The voice-containing derivative files creatures/normal-*.wav, armored-*.wav,
hive-*.wav and immolator-*.wav retain this attribution and CC BY 4.0 licensing.

"Zombie dies 1" by Breviceps:
https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/445982/
CC0 1.0; cropped, normalized, pitch-varied and mixed for each death voice.

Hellhound barking (real dogs): "Barking dogs" #0288, Denis Chardonnet / BigSoundBank.
